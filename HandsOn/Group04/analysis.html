2. Source and generated dataset licensing

The selected sources are published by Ayuntamiento de Madrid through its Open Data portal. Both official records identify Dirección General de Sostenibilidad y Control Ambiental as the responsible department. The sources and license statements below were checked on 4 October 2026.

Publisher and rights holder

Ayuntamiento de Madrid is the publisher and the presumed source rights holder. The records do not identify a separate rights holder; this attribution is based on the municipal source and its general reuse conditions.

Source license and reuse

Both datasets use CC BY 4.0, which permits redistribution and adaptation, including commercial use. RDF publication must credit the source, link the license and indicate changes, without implying municipal endorsement or imposing incompatible restrictions. Supplied update and reuse metadata will be retained. The exact download date of the local copies was not recorded.

Potential license for the generated RDF dataset

We propose CC BY 4.0 for the generated RDF, allowing further reuse while preserving source attribution. Group 04 will be credited for the transformation and its original contributions; source rights remain with their holders. Publication will include links to both source records and a description of the changes made.

Suggested attribution: “Source: Ayuntamiento de Madrid, Dirección General de Sostenibilidad y Control Ambiental. Calidad del aire: Estaciones de control and Datos en tiempo real (CC BY 4.0). RDF transformation by Group 04.” This text will accompany the source and license links above.
